## 🎮 What is This?

**Madden-27-Mod-Manager** is a complete mod management solution for **Madden NFL 27**. It supports .mod files, providing drag & drop import, load order control, conflict detection, and roster editing. Perfect for enhancing graphics, gameplay, and franchise mode.

## ✨ Features

- **Drag & Drop Import** – Add .mod files instantly
- **Load Order Control** – Organize mods to avoid conflicts
- **Conflict Detection** – Automatically detects overlapping files
- **Roster Editor** – Edit player attributes, ratings, and contracts
- **Player Editor** – Modify appearance, equipment, and stats
- **Franchise Tool** – Edit team budgets, league settings, and draft picks
- **Realism Mods** – Enhanced AI, injury logic, and player behavior
- **Visual Mods** – Realistic faces, uniforms, stadiums, and scoreboards
- **Preset Loader** – Load reusable mod presets easily
- **Profile Management** – Save and load different mod configurations
- **Restore Points** – One-click rollback to previous states

1. Click the download button above
2. Extract the archive

5. Drag & drop your mods or browse the Mod Store
